About

My research in machine learning for medical imaging focuses on inverse problems and learning from limited or degraded data. I completed my M.Sc. in Data Engineering and Analytics at the Technical University of Munich (TUM) and carried out my thesis research as a visiting graduate student at the Psychiatry Neuroimaging Laboratory, Brigham and Women's Hospital, Harvard Medical School. I am currently looking for research positions in academia or industry. Feel free to reach out.
